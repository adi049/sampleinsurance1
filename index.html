NCB (No-Claim Bonus) guide

  • Ranges 20% → 50% on own-damage premium for claim-free years.
  • Transferable when you change insurer or buy a new vehicle (same class).
  • One own-damage claim usually resets it — we help you decide small claims.
Tip: Renew before expiry to keep NCB + avoid inspection hassles.

IDV quick explainer

IDV (Insured Declared Value) ≈ current market value minus depreciation. Too low saves premium but cuts claim payout; too high wastes premium. Our quote flow suggests a fair band based on registration year.

0–1 yr old

~95% of ex-showroom

3 yrs old

~70–75% band

5+ yrs old

~50% or agreed value
